# Copy voice

Second person, short declaratives, one specific number where one exists. The headline makes the claim, the lede explains how, the CTA names the action. Sound like the owner on a good day, not like an agency.

## Headlines: one turn each
The accent phrase (italic or highlighted) is the payoff, not a random word.

- "Love is the greatest art, *and we are its designers.*"
- "Have a budget in mind? *Let's design around it.*"
- "You're charging $3,000 for a $10,000 wedding. *We fix that in 90 days.*"
- "Same wedding. *Different price.*"
- "Don't take our word for it. *Take theirs.*"
- "Everything you'll receive to charge premium prices, *itemised.*"
- "We print the *big stuff.*" (rotating: small stuff, seating charts, welcome signs, weird shapes, menus, floor wraps, custom cutouts, realtor signs, foamboard, marketing materials, banners, backdrops)
- "See your price *before you blink.*"
- "If you can think it, *we can print it.*"
- "Affordable. Fast. *Actually reliable.*"
- "See it big. *Pay what you saw.*"

## Ledes
One or two sentences, no more than 52 characters wide, muted colour. Say the mechanism.

- "No emails, no waiting. Drop in your artwork, choose a size and we'll show it next to a real person so you know exactly what you're getting."
- "Before we build anything, we help you book as many weddings as we can from the inquiries you already have."

## Kickers
Three to five words, uppercase, tracked. Name the section's job or the audience: "For home-based wedding decor businesses", "Instant quote", "Love notes", "Brampton & the GTA", "Who it's for", "Everything included".

## Buttons
Verb first, outcome second, never "Submit" or "Learn more": "Start your inquiry", "Get my instant quote", "Apply for a free Dead Lead Audit", "Go to our budget planner →", "Add to cart & checkout". Ghost button is the softer path: "See our work", "I need design help →", "See what we build →".

## Reassurance lines
A ✦ then one sentence under the CTAs: "We show you the thousands of dollars just sitting in your inbox before you spend a dollar." "No file? Our in-house designers bring your vision to life from $49."

## Claim bubbles and proof captions
Claim in three words, proof in one line: "Super affordable / from $3 per sq ft, no setup fees", "1–2 day turnaround / 48h rush on anything", "4.9 on Google / 300+ reviews across the GTA".

## FAQ
Questions in the customer's words, answers in two or three sentences that end the objection: file types, turnaround, "can you design it", delivery, "is the online price final".

## SEO copy
One plain paragraph per site that names the service, the city and the surrounding towns in natural English, repeated in condensed form in the footer. Title tag pattern: "Service in City & Region | Brand". Meta description under 160 characters with the mechanism ("Upload your artwork, pick a size, see your price instantly").

## Things to avoid
Exclamation marks in headlines, "we are passionate about", stacked adjectives, em dashes in UI copy, stock phrases like "elevate" and "bespoke", and any claim without a number next to it when a number exists.
