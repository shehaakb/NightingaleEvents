Questions

Good to know before you order.

What file types do you accept?

PDF, PNG, JPG, AI, EPS and PSD. For best results, send vector files or images at 100 to 150 DPI at final print size. If you're unsure, upload what you have and we'll check it for free before printing.

How fast is turnaround?

Standard orders are ready in 2 to 3 business days. Rush orders are ready within 48 hours. Large runs or custom finishing may add a day, and we'll always tell you before you pay.

Can you design it for me?

Yes. Our in-house graphic designers create banners, signs, backdrops and logos from scratch or from your rough idea. Design starts at $49 and includes two rounds of revisions.

Do you deliver?

Pick up free at our Brampton studio, or choose delivery anywhere in the GTA at checkout. Orders over $250 ship free within the GTA.

Is the online price final?

Yes. The instant quote includes printing and standard finishing. The only additions are tax and optional delivery, both shown at checkout.
