Questions

How fast can you print my order?

Most orders are ready in 2–3 business days. Rush service gets banners, signs, flyers and stickers done in 24 hours. Business cards and specialty finishes may need an extra day.

Is delivery really free?

Yes. Orders over $50 are delivered free anywhere in the GTA, from Oakville to Scarborough. Smaller orders can be picked up in Brampton or delivered for a flat $8.

What file types do you accept?

PDF is best. We also take AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, JPG and TIFF. Aim for 150 DPI at final size for large format and 300 DPI for cards and flyers. Not sure? Upload it and our checker will tell you.

I don't have a design. Can you make one?

Absolutely. Our in-house designers create logos, layouts and full campaigns. Single-piece layouts start at $49 and you approve a proof before we print.

Do you price-match?

Send us any written quote from a GTA print shop and we'll match or beat it on comparable specs.
